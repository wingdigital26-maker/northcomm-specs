How Serial Searches Work

When you enter a serial number, the page queries a public, read-only database hosted on Supabase to retrieve the matching report. These reports contain hardware test results and technical specifications only. Zero personal data is collected, requested, or stored.

Requesting a Missing Report

If a report isn't on file, clicking the request link simply opens a blank draft in your device's default email client. Nothing is sent automatically. You control what to write and whether to send it. We only receive information if you explicitly choose to email us.
